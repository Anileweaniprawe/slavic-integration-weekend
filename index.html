WHAT IS INTEGRATION WEEKEND?

Integration Weekend is a three-day trip where your Erasmus really begins.

We’ve been organising Integration Weekends since 2010 - and this year’s Wild West edition is our 30th. Over the years, we’ve welcomed hundreds of international students and helped them start their semester in the best possible way!

It’s all about meeting new people, building friendships, and feeling part of an international community from the beginning of your Erasmus. Through team activities, the wildest parties, and a lot of shared laughs, you’ll connect with students from all over the world in a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere.
